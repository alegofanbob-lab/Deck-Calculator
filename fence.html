About these estimates
What updates automatically

Wood fence pricing tracks the same Federal Reserve Softwood Lumber Producer Price Index (FRED series WPU0811) used by our deck calculator, refreshed on the 2nd of every month.

What's reviewed manually

Chain-link, vinyl, and aluminum fencing have no public live-data source — nothing tracks these as structured data anywhere. These figures are reviewed roughly every 3-6 months.

What's not included

This covers fence materials, standard post-setting, and basic labor. It does not include: removal of an existing fence, extensive grading/excavation for sloped yards, or HOA-specific style requirements.

Setting posts in concrete

Every fence post needs a concrete footing. Use our concrete calculator to estimate how many bags you'll need based on your post count and hole size.
